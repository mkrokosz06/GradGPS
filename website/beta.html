Get the beta

Get GradGPS on your iPhone.

GradGPS is in open beta, which means it installs through TestFlight — Apple’s own free app for trying apps before they hit the App Store. It costs nothing and takes about a minute.

  1. Install TestFlight from the App Store, if you don’t already have it. It’s free and made by Apple.
  2. Come back and tap the button below. It opens TestFlight and adds GradGPS. This is the step people miss — the link only works once TestFlight is installed.
  3. Open GradGPS and upload your transcript. In LionPATH: Academic Records → Unofficial Transcript. Your plan builds from there.

Open in TestFlight

Free · no credit card · delete your account and data any time from inside the app.

On a laptop?

TestFlight only runs on iPhone and iPad, so this link won’t do anything on a desktop browser. Open gradgps.com/beta on your phone instead.

A scannable QR code is coming to this page shortly.

On Android?

GradGPS is iPhone-only while the beta runs — there is no Android build yet, and pretending otherwise would waste your time. Android is on the roadmap.

Send us a note and we’ll tell you when it lands.

What you’re signing up for

A beta. Things break, some majors are modelled better than others, and you may find a requirement the audit reads wrong. When that happens, tell us — that is genuinely how most of the catalog bugs have been found so far.

Read how the audit works and where it stops short, or the privacy policy if you want to know exactly what happens to your transcript before you upload it.
