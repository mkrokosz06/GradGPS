Finance at Penn State: 4‑year plan & entrance requirements

Penn State’s suggested 8-semester route through the Finance B.S. at University Park, 120 credits in all, with every course titled and the Entrance to Major courses marked.

Entrance to Major

The courses you need before you can formally enter the major.

  • One of: ENGL 15 Rhetoric and Composition or ENGL 30H Honors Rhetoric and Composition or ESL 15 ESL Composition for American Academic Communication II or ENGL 137H Rhetoric and Civic Life I or CAS 137H Rhetoric and Civic Life I
  • One of: MATH 110 Techniques of Calculus I or MATH 140 Calculus With Analytic Geometry I or MATH 140B Calculus and Biology I
  • One of: SCM 200 Introduction to Statistics for Business or SCM 200H Honors Introduction to Statistics for Business or STAT 200 Elementary Statistics
  • One of: ECON 102 Introductory Microeconomic Analysis and Policy or ECON 102H Introductory Microeconomic Analysis and Policy (Honors)
  • One of: ACCTG 211 Financial and Managerial Accounting for Decision Making or ACCTG 211H Financial and Managerial Accounting for Decision Making or (ACCTG 201 Introduction to Financial Accounting and ACCTG 202 Introduction to Managerial Accounting)
  • One of: MGMT 301 Basic Management Concepts or MGMT 301H Basic Management Concepts (Honors) or MGMT 301W Basic Management Concepts
  • One of: MKTG 301 Principles of Marketing or MKTG 301H Principles of Marketing (Honors) or MKTG 301W Principles of Marketing
  • One of: FIN 301 Corporation Finance or FIN 301H Corporation Finance (Honors)

Each course needs a grade of C or better.

The bulletin gives more than one GPA figure depending on your situation, so check the official section for the one that applies to you.

There are other conditions too, such as credit windows, application deadlines or space limits, that aren’t summarised here.

Read the full entrance section on the Penn State bulletin.
