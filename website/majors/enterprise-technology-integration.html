Enterprise Technology Integration at Penn State: 4‑year plan & entrance requirements

Penn State’s suggested 9-semester route through the Enterprise Technology Integration B.S. at University Park, 124 credits in all, with every course titled and the Entrance to Major courses marked.

Entrance to Major

The courses you need before you can formally enter the major.

  • One of: ETI 100 Introduction to Enterprise Technology Integration or HCDD 113S Foundations of Human-Centered Design and Development FYS or HCDD 113 Foundations of Human-Centered Design and Development or CYBER 100 Computer Systems Literacy or CYBER 100S Computer Systems Literacy or IST 110 Information, People and Technology or A-I 100
  • One of: IST 140 Introduction to Application Development or CMPSC 121 Introduction to Programming Techniques or CMPSC 131 Programming and Computation I: Fundamentals
  • IST 210 Organization of Data
  • IST 220 Networking and Telecommunications
  • One of: IST 242 Intermediate & Object-Oriented Application Development or CMPSC 122 Intermediate Programming or CMPSC 132 Programming and Computation II: Data Structures
  • One of: STAT 200 Elementary Statistics or SCM 200 Introduction to Statistics for Business

Each course needs a grade of C or better.

Minimum cumulative GPA: 2.00.

Read the full entrance section on the Penn State bulletin.
