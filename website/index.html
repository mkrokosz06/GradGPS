How it works

Three steps from transcript to graduation plan.

No spreadsheets, no guessing which requirements you’ve already knocked out, no waiting two weeks for an advising appointment to find out.

1

Upload your transcript

Export your unofficial transcript from LionPATH and drop it in. GradGPS parses every course, credit, and grade in seconds.

2

Pick your major

Search any Penn State major and add your concentration. Switching majors? Re-run the audit and instantly see what still counts.

3

Follow your route

Get a full degree audit and a semester-by-semester timeline to graduation that updates every time your record changes.

What’s inside

An advisor that has actually read the bulletin.

GradGPS audits against the real Penn State catalog — not a simplified copy — so the plan it gives you is one the registrar would agree with.

A real degree audit

Every requirement group in your major, checked against your record — including alternatives like MATH 110 or MATH 140, and credit pools that let you choose.

Gen eds, handled

Arts, sciences, humanities, cultures, quantification, writing-intensive credits — all eight domains tracked, with the one-course-one-category rules applied correctly.

Official plan sequencing

Where Penn State publishes a Suggested Academic Plan, your timeline follows it — prerequisites in order, credits balanced, reflowed around what you’ve already taken.

Know your professors

RateMyProfessors ratings appear right on each course, so when your plan says “take STAT 200,” you also know who to take it with.

Always current

Change your major, add a class, drop one — the audit and timeline rebuild instantly. Your plan is never a stale PDF from freshman orientation.

Private by default

Sign in with Google. Your transcript belongs to you — it’s used to build your plan and nothing else, and you can delete it any time.
