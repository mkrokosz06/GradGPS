Directions

Three steps from transcript to graduation.

No spreadsheets, no guessing which requirements you’ve already knocked out, no waiting two weeks for an advising appointment to find out.

Start

Upload your transcript

Grab the app on TestFlight, export your unofficial transcript from LionPATH (Academic Records → Unofficial Transcript — free and instant), and drop it in. Every course, credit, and grade parsed in seconds.

Then

Pick your major

Search any Penn State major and add your concentration. Switching majors? Re-run the audit and instantly see what still counts.

Arrive

Follow your route

Your AI advisor hands you a full degree audit plus a semester-by-semester timeline to graduation, recalculated every time your record changes.

Live example

Watch a real route come together.

Every stop is a semester — completed ones checked off, a “you are here” marker on the term in progress, and a dashed line running all the way to your graduation pin.

Route: Enterprise Technology Integration, B.S. 97 / 120 CR · ETA MAY 2027

Map legend

An AI advisor that has actually read the bulletin.

No hallucinated course lists. The AI reads your transcript PDF, matches every course against the real Penn State catalog — not a simplified copy — and plans your remaining semesters, so the route it gives you is one the registrar would agree with.

A real degree audit

Every requirement group in your major, checked against your record — including alternatives like MATH 110 or MATH 140, and credit pools that let you choose.

Gen eds, handled

Arts, sciences, humanities, cultures, quantification, writing-intensive — all eight domains tracked, with the one-course-one-category rules applied correctly.

Official plan sequencing

Where Penn State publishes a Suggested Academic Plan, your timeline follows it — prerequisites in order, credits balanced, reflowed around what you’ve already taken.

Know your professors

Professor ratings appear right on each course, so when your route says “take STAT 200,” you also know who to take it with.

Reroutes in real time

Change your major, add a class, drop one — your AI advisor rebuilds the audit and timeline instantly. Your plan is never a stale PDF from freshman orientation.

Private by default

Sign in with Google. Your transcript belongs to you — it’s used to build your plan and nothing else, and you can delete it any time.

Support

Hit a detour? Tell us.

Wrong course on your timeline, a transcript that won’t parse, a requirement the audit got wrong — send it over and we’ll take a look. Include your major if it’s about the audit; that’s usually the first thing we check.

Replies go straight to the email you enter, usually within a day or two.
