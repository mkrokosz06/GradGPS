Get the app

Get GradGPS on your iPhone.

GradGPS is free on the App Store. It takes about a minute to install and set up.

  1. Get GradGPS from the App Store. It’s free — no subscription, no in-app purchases.
  2. Sign in with Apple, Google, or your email.
  3. Upload your transcript. In LionPATH: Academic Records → Unofficial Transcript. Your plan builds from there.

Download on the App Store

Free · no credit card · delete your account and data any time from inside the app.

On a laptop?

The button opens GradGPS’s App Store page, but you install it on your phone. Search GradGPS in the App Store on your iPhone, or open gradgps.com/download there.

On Android?

GradGPS is iPhone-only for now — there is no Android build yet, and pretending otherwise would waste your time. Android is on the roadmap.

Send us a note and we’ll tell you when it lands.

Before you upload

GradGPS is new, and some majors are modelled better than others — you may find a requirement the audit reads wrong. When that happens, tell us; that is genuinely how most of the catalog bugs have been found so far.

Read how the audit works, or the privacy policy if you want to know exactly what happens to your transcript before you upload it.
